| **Novelty Feature**                     | **What's Novel About It?**                                                                                                            |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Shortage Radar**                      | Predicts future stock shortages before they happen instead of alerting after stock is low.                                            |
| **Why-Procurement Explainer**           | Explains in plain English *why* a purchase/manufacturing order was automatically created.                                             |
| **Inventory Digital Twin**              | Simulates the entire factory and inventory to test "What if demand doubles?" or "What if a supplier is delayed?"                      |
| **Interactive Supply Chain Risk Graph** | Displays products, suppliers, BoMs, and work centers as an interactive network showing how one problem affects everything else.       |
| **Bottleneck Seer**                     | Predicts future production bottlenecks before delays occur.                                                                           |
| **Auto-Schedule Optimizer**             | Automatically creates the best production schedule based on deadlines, machine capacity, and materials.                               |
| **Visual Stock Audits (AI + Camera)**   | Uses images from a phone/camera to detect missing, damaged, or misplaced inventory automatically.                                     |
| **Order Promise Engine**                | Calculates an accurate delivery date by considering inventory, suppliers, and production capacity.                                    |
| **Exception Inbox**                     | Shows only critical issues that need attention instead of making users search through hundreds of records.                            |
| **Self-Healing Workflow**               | Automatically reschedules production or creates alternative plans when shortages or delays occur.                                     |
| **Timeline Replay**                     | Lets users replay every event (order → purchase → manufacturing → delivery) like a timeline to understand what happened.              |
| **Factory Health Score**                | Gives one overall score (0–100) representing the health of the entire factory based on delays, stock, production, and pending orders. |
| **Lead-Time Drift Detector**            | Detects when suppliers start delivering slower than usual and warns users before it impacts production.                               |
